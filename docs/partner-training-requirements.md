# Partner and small-group personal training — deferred

Recorded on November 3, 2026. Requirements only; do not implement as part of Team management or Announcements.

- A Coach selects multiple assigned Clients for one shared session.
- Each participant completes their own intake and readiness questions.
- Generate coordinated exercise order and station timing where appropriate.
- Individualize each prescription's loads, repetitions, effort targets and substitutions from that Client's assessments, goals, restrictions and performance history.
- Use safer alternatives where required; never force identical exercises.
- Keep prescriptions, results, progression and private information separate by Client.
- Allow the authorized Coach to view and edit the prescriptions together. Each Client sees only their own prescription and results.
- Preserve one-to-one training and existing scheduling.

Review scheduling/data isolation and client-safety requirements in a separate release before implementation.
